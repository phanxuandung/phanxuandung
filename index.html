Phan Xuan Dung.

I study Vietnam’s place in the world and how the legacies of war shape politics, memory, and reconciliation.

I am a PhD candidate in International Relations at the Australian National University and a Senior Research Officer in the Vietnam Studies Programme at ISEAS–Yusof Ishak Institute.

01 Research

War’s afterlives.
Vietnam’s international relations.

i.

Memory, justice & reconciliation

My doctoral research examines Vietnamese agency in the memory politics of Agent Orange, tracing struggles over recognition and accountability across political, scientific, legal, and cultural settings.

ii.

Vietnam & the international order

I examine Vietnam’s foreign relations, its responses to great-power competition, and the relationship between domestic politics and international strategy.

iii.

Mekong politics & environmental security

My work also explores geopolitical competition in the Mekong basin and the environmental and maritime challenges facing Vietnam.
